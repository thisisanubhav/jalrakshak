# Future impact framework

JalRakshak has not yet demonstrated social impact. A future supervised pilot should measure whether the workflow improves decisions without creating duplicate burden.

## Measurement-quality outcomes

- Percentage of visits with two agreeing readings.
- Percentage requiring a third reading.
- Percentage excluded because calibration/context metadata is absent or incompatible.
- Time required to complete and document a visit.

## Continuity and evidence outcomes

- Percentage of repeat visits where prior source history is available at the point of measurement.
- Offline records retained successfully until export or approved transfer.
- Duplicate or incomplete records compared with the existing workflow.
- Investigation packages accepted as useful by the receiving coordinator or laboratory role.

## Decision outcomes

- Cases where source history changes a legitimate next action: repeat measurement, sanitary inspection, or approved sample referral.
- False or ambiguous EC changes attributable to season, temperature, device drift, probe fouling, or sampling method.
- Whether calibration and device maintenance ownership is operationally feasible.

## Guardrails

- Never measure “safe water detected” or “contamination detected.”
- Keep synthetic, manual, physical-sensor, public, and laboratory-confirmed data distinct.
- Precommit pilot decision criteria before collecting results.
- Stop or pivot again if history does not affect decisions or the added workflow creates unacceptable duplicate entry.
