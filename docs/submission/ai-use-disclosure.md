# AI-assistance disclosure

## Verified use

AI coding assistance was used during JalRakshak’s development to help:

- implement and revise application code;
- draft and expand automated tests;
- identify edge cases and organize QA;
- structure research and documentation;
- draft bilingual interface text for review; and
- prepare release and submission materials.

AI output was not accepted as evidence on its own. Material code changes were reviewed in version-control diffs and checked with linting, TypeScript, unit tests, production builds, Playwright browser tests, and live-deployment QA. Scientific and institutional claims were traced to authoritative government, WHO, BIS, or peer-reviewed sources in the project’s [source register](../research/sources.md). Uncertainty and missing direct stakeholder evidence are stated explicitly.

## Human decisions

The human project owner defined the problem, scope, competition goals, scientific boundaries, product direction, merge authorization, and release decisions. The owner also required the project to remain software-only and prohibited unsupported water-safety or contaminant-detection claims.

## Important limitations

- AI assistance does not substitute for scientific validation, institutional approval, accredited laboratory work, or direct stakeholder research.
- No claim is made that all code or prose was written manually.
- No AI-generated stakeholder interviews, field observations, impact outcomes, or government feedback are presented as real.
- If the competition form requires a percentage of AI-assisted work, that value is **PENDING OWNER CONFIRMATION** because the repository history does not provide a reliable, competition-defined percentage.
