# AI-assistance disclosure

## Verified use

AI coding assistance was used during JalRakshak’s development to help:

- implement and revise application code;
- draft and expand automated tests;
- identify edge cases and organize QA;
- structure research and documentation;
- draft bilingual interface text for review; and
- prepare release and submission materials.

AI output is not evidence on its own. Verification has included version-control diff reviews, linting, TypeScript checks, unit tests, production builds, browser tests, and local/shared-server QA. These checks do not establish that every output is correct. Scientific and institutional claims were traced to authoritative government, WHO, BIS, or peer-reviewed sources in the project’s [source register](../research/sources.md). Uncertainty and missing direct stakeholder evidence are stated explicitly.

## Human decisions

The human project owner defined the problem, scope, competition goals, scientific boundaries, product direction, merge authorization, and release decisions. The owner directed the software workflow and optional ESP32 integration, and prohibited unsupported water-safety or contaminant-detection claims.

## Important limitations

- AI assistance does not substitute for scientific validation, institutional approval, accredited laboratory work, or direct stakeholder research.
- No claim is made that all code or prose was written manually.
- No AI-generated stakeholder interviews, field observations, impact outcomes, or government feedback are presented as real.
