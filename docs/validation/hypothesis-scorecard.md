## Update rules

- `SUPPORTED`: repeated, specific behavioral evidence across relevant roles with no decisive contradiction.
- `MIXED`: meaningful support and contradiction, or role/site dependence.
- `CONTRADICTED`: observed/current workflow evidence defeats the assumption.
- `UNTESTED`: no direct evidence, vague preference, hypothetical answer or desk evidence only.
- Confidence reflects evidence quality and convergence, not participant count alone.
- Add anonymized interview IDs, not names, to direct-evidence cells.
