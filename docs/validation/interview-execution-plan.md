# Direct validation execution plan

**Current state: DIRECT STAKEHOLDER VALIDATION PENDING.** This plan is for the project team to execute with real participants; Codex has not interviewed anyone.

## Who to approach first

1. A block/district DWSD water-quality contact or implementation partner who can authorize learning conversations and identify active field users.
2. Two Jal Sahiyas/current FTK users who performed a recent test.
3. A sample collector or block water-quality coordinator.
4. A receiving laboratory technician/in-charge.
5. Additional field users, coordinator/engineer and VWSC/NGO participant until role coverage and saturation.

Do not cold-contact field workers in a way that implies government endorsement. Use an authorized introduction where possible.

## Target and timing

- 8–12 conversations; 15–20 minutes each.
- At least 3 recent hands-on field users and 1 receiving-laboratory participant.
- Conduct discovery questions before the concept test.
- If the first laboratory/decision conversations establish that EC history cannot affect any legitimate action, pause and evaluate a STOP/PIVOT AGAIN decision rather than accumulating supportive opinions.

## Introduction and consent

Suggested introduction:

> “I am working on a student social-impact research project studying how rural water-quality measurements are recorded and followed up in Jharkhand. This conversation is for workflow learning, not an audit or sales pitch. Participation is voluntary. I would like to take anonymized notes, without names or personal contact details. You may skip any question or stop at any time. Is that acceptable?”

For recording, request separate explicit permission and explain storage/deletion. If permission is absent, take anonymized notes only.

## What not to claim

- Do not say JalRakshak detects contamination or makes water safe.
- Do not imply affiliation with JJM, DWSD, WQMIS or Jhar-Jal.
- Do not say a device has been selected or validated.
- Do not promise deployment, funding, employment, faster laboratory service or government adoption.
- Do not describe desk evidence as feedback from workers.

## Note capture and anonymization

- Assign IDs such as `FW-01`, `LAB-01`, `COORD-01`.
- Store role and organization type; record district/block only when voluntarily provided and necessary.
- Keep recruitment contacts in a separate approved private system, not Git.
- Separate verbatim quotes (with permission), paraphrase, observation and interpretation.
- Remove private source coordinates, names, phone numbers, credentials and personnel disputes.
- Use [interview-template.md](interview-template.md) for the sanitized record.

## After each conversation

1. Complete notes within 24 hours.
2. Add specific evidence/contradiction to H1–H10 with interview ID.
3. Keep status `UNTESTED` for vague preference or hypothetical answers.
4. Record alternative explanations and interviewer bias.
5. Identify one follow-up question for another role.

## Synthesis

- Group evidence by actual behavior/decision, not feature enthusiasm.
- Create a workflow map from observed/recent examples.
- Identify which role owns each step and where records are duplicated.
- Report negative cases and disagreements.
- Update [hypothesis-scorecard.md](hypothesis-scorecard.md) and apply [decision-criteria.md](decision-criteria.md) unchanged.

## Stopping criteria

Stop interviews and decide when:

- all minimum roles are covered and two consecutive conversations add no material workflow insight; or
- a decisive STOP condition is corroborated by the relevant decision/laboratory role; or
- access/consent is insufficient for credible evidence.

Do not claim saturation from convenience sampling among one organization or role.
