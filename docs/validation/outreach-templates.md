# Workflow-learning outreach templates

Adapt the language and channel appropriately. These messages request research conversations; they do not imply endorsement, sales or deployment.

## Jal Sahiya / field worker

Namaste. I am working on a student social-impact research project studying how rural water-quality tests are recorded and followed up in Jharkhand. I would value 15–20 minutes to understand your current field workflow, including repeat testing, record keeping and connectivity challenges. This is not an inspection or sales call, and I will not ask for personal or confidential information. Participation is voluntary. Would you be willing to speak at a convenient time?

## DWSD official / coordinator

Namaste. I am conducting a student research study on the existing rural water-quality measurement and confirmation workflow in Jharkhand. Before proposing any technology, I want to understand how Jhar-Jal/WQMIS, FTKs, sample collection, repeated source testing and laboratory follow-up work in practice. Could I request a 15–20 minute workflow-learning conversation? I will not represent the project as government-affiliated, and any notes used in the study will be anonymized unless explicitly agreed otherwise.

## Laboratory professional

Namaste. I am studying how rural drinking-water field results become laboratory confirmation requests in Jharkhand. I would appreciate 15–20 minutes to learn what information accompanies samples, common quality or identification problems, and whether source history ever affects laboratory intake or follow-up. This is a student research conversation, not a product sale or evaluation of your laboratory. No confidential sample or personal information is requested.

## NGO / researcher

Hello. I am working on a student social-impact project testing a specific assumption: whether repeatable, offline conductivity history could add value to the existing Jharkhand FTK/Jhar-Jal/WQMIS workflow. I am deliberately not assuming that it does. Could I request 15–20 minutes to understand the field workflow you have observed, the dominant bottlenecks and where another tool would duplicate existing work? Findings will distinguish direct evidence from desk research and will be anonymized as appropriate.

## Follow-up request

Thank you for the conversation. One point would benefit from the perspective of someone who personally [performs field tests / receives confirmation samples / manages WQMIS records]. If appropriate and permitted, could you suggest the correct role or official channel to approach? Please do not share anyone’s personal contact information without their consent.
