# Stakeholder validation plan

**Direct stakeholder validation: NOT YET COMPLETED.** Role selection is based on Round 3 desk evidence, not confirmed availability or interest.

## Central hypothesis

> Trained Jharkhand rural-water field workers have a meaningful operational need for repeatable offline EC measurements and source-level historical comparison that is not adequately addressed by the current Jhar-Jal / FTK / WQMIS workflow.

## Testable assumptions

| ID | Assumption |
|---|---|
| H1 | Repeated measurements of the same source are operationally useful. |
| H2 | Workers currently have difficulty comparing today’s measurement with previous measurements. |
| H3 | Persistent EC change can influence a legitimate next action: repeat measurement, sanitary inspection or approved sample confirmation. |
| H4 | Offline retention is a real field problem. |
| H5 | Calibration/device traceability is a meaningful problem. |
| H6 | Duplicate stabilized readings improve confidence enough to justify their extra effort. |
| H7 | Existing official systems do not already solve these needs adequately. |
| H8 | A companion tool would not impose unacceptable duplicate entry. |
| H9 | A real institution/person can own calibration, maintenance and replacement. |
| H10 | Decision value justifies cost, time and training burden. |

## Minimum useful stakeholder set

Target **8–12 conversations**, stopping earlier if strong saturation or a decisive contradiction emerges. A practical target is 3–4 field users, 2 programme coordinators/engineers, 2 laboratory/sample-flow participants and 1–2 governance/implementation partners.

| Selected role | Why needed | Assumptions informed | What they should not decide alone |
|---|---|---|---|
| Authorized Jal Sahiya or current FTK user (3–4) | First-hand field sequence, repeat testing, app/connectivity burden, workarounds and maintenance capacity. Jharkhand programme evidence confirms these roles can perform FTK/H2S and sanitary work, but not that every Jal Sahiya does so. | H1, H2, H4, H6, H7, H8, H9, H10 | Scientific validity of EC, regulatory thresholds, statewide representativeness or laboratory method. |
| DWSD sample collector or block water-quality coordinator (1–2) | Connects source visits, sample identity, current digital systems and escalation; can explain local authorization. | H1–H5, H7, H8 | Whether a low-cost sensor is accurate without validation; procurement decision for the entire state. |
| District water-quality coordinator or PHED/DWSD engineer (1) | Tests whether EC history could influence an authorized operational decision and whether integration/ownership is plausible. | H3, H7–H10 | Community usability without worker evidence; contaminant inference from EC. |
| Laboratory technician/in-charge receiving rural samples (1–2) | Reveals what evidence is useful at intake, common sample defects, confirmation criteria and whether EC history changes prioritization or test selection. | H3, H5–H7, H10 | Field-worker burden or willingness; public-health action outside their mandate. |
| VWSC member or NGO implementation partner (optional 1–2) | Clarifies local governance, worker support, source identity, community communication and what happens after results. Include only if active in water testing, not merely available. | H1, H4, H8–H10 | Instrument performance or official laboratory decisions. |

The JJM framework supports the terms FTK user, sample collector, laboratory official, DWSM and VWSC; Jharkhand sources support Jal Sahiya. “Paani Samiti” should not be presumed locally—the 2024 Jharkhand assessment says the state relies on VWSCs, Jal Sahiyas and sample takers.

## Sampling and evidence rules

- Seek variation in district/block, source type, experience and connectivity; do not claim statistical representativeness.
- Prefer people who recently performed or received an actual test, not only supervisors describing policy.
- Ask for the last real workflow before discussing the concept.
- Separate observed behavior, participant report and researcher interpretation.
- No names, phone numbers, personal addresses or employee IDs in the repository.
- Record disagreement and negative cases; do not count polite interest as workflow value.
- One person’s statement cannot establish statewide practice.

## Evidence threshold

Direct evidence exists only when an actual conversation/observation is documented using the interview template. Government manuals, publications and this plan remain **desk evidence**.
